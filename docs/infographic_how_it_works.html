Every other public model rates teams on what the scoreboard said. This one rates them on how they move the ball to score — the geometry of their scoring chains, not the result.

The prediction pipeline

01
Read every chain
Each possession chain from every game, 2021–2026.
289,469 chains
1.9M events
02
Map to the ground
The ground is a 5×3 grid. Every scoring chain becomes a path between cells, ending in a shot.
15 cells + GOAL
03
Build a fingerprint
Each team gets a movement fingerprint. Their own chains count up; the opponent's count down, viewed from the other end.
56,016 scoring chains
04
Subtract the two
Home fingerprint minus away fingerprint. Whoever comes out positive is the tip.
The raw signal decides
05
Size the margin
That difference, plus a recent-form rating, converted into a predicted margin.
±26.8 pts avg error

A chain, as the model sees it

Four transitions: three movements between cells, then the shot. Each is weighted by how close it was to the goal. Two teams can kick the same score and leave completely different fingerprints — that difference is what the model reads.
