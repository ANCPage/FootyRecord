Methodology & System Mathematics

This document explains the mathematical and algorithmic foundation of FootyRecord: from tracking ball transition chains across the pitch, to rotating matchup vectors and updating team ELO ratings.

1. Possession Chain Decay Model

Transitions that culminate in a scoring shot are analyzed. Since actions closer to the goal are highly predictive of success, transitions are weighted using a backward decay factor.

w(ei) = d n - i
  • ei: The transition edge at step i in a chain of length n.
  • d: The decay factor, typically set to 0.90 (adjustable between 0.50 and 1.00).
  • n: The total number of transitions in the chain, ending with SCORE.
  • w(ei): The weight added to that transition edge. The final kick/handpass to a scoring position (i = n) receives a weight of 1.00 (d0), while preceding moves decay exponentially.

To compare opposing teams, Team B's offensive profile is rotated 180 degrees into Team A's frame of reference. The rotation function is:

R(row, col) = (2 - row, 4 - col)

Thus, Team A's center-forward transition (C2 → D2) is directly compared with Team B's center-defensive transition rotated (C2 → B2) in their respective profiles.

3. Matchup Vector Delta

The tactical matchup score represents the net difference between Team A's and Team B's average transition rates over a sliding window.

Δ(e) = MA(e) - MB(R(e))
Net Delta = Σ Δ(e)

The Combined Score integrates this Net Delta with ELO differences:

Combined Score = Net Delta + wElo × (ELOA - ELOB) / 100
  • Combined Score > 0: Team A (Home) is predicted to win.
  • Combined Score < 0: Team B (Away) is predicted to win.
  • Predicted Margin: |Combined Score × 12.0| points.

4. ELO Engine & Margin Multipliers

Ratings update dynamically after each game. Expected scores are calculated from pre-game ratings, and the final adjustment is scaled by a margin multiplier.

EA = 1 / (1 + 10(ELOB - ELOA)/400)
ΔELOA = K × Mult × (SA - EA)
Mult = min(3.0, max(0.5, |Δactual| / 10.0 + 1.0))
  • EA: Expectation of victory for Team A (between 0.0 and 1.0).
  • SA: Actual result outcome (1.0 for Team A win, 0.5 for draw, 0.0 for Loss).
  • K: ELO update weight (default K = 32).
  • Mult: Multiplier based on the net actual margin of transition success in the match, preventing small wins from causing oversized rating updates.

5. Tactical Visualization & Interpretation

When simulating matchups, the engine overlays both teams' historical networks. By calculating MA(e) - MB(R(e)), we discover where Team A has a higher transition rate (rendered in Team A's color) and where Team B dominates (rendered in Team B's color). The arrow thickness corresponds to the size of the delta. Selecting playmakers highlights their specific contributions, letting users identify who initiates transitions and who finishes them in the SCORE zone.
