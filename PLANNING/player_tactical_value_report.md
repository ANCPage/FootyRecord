# Tactical Player Value Report: The 'Difficult Positions' Experts
Based on the 2025 H&A Season.

## Methodology: Expected Value Added (EVA)
We mapped every single possession and disposal to the normalized A1-E3 grid. 
For every grid zone, we calculated the historical league-average probability of that chain resulting in a SCORE.
For example, a chain passing through deep defense (A2) might only have a 6% chance to score, while the attacking pocket (E3) has a 45% chance.

Every time a player touches the ball, they are measured against that zone's baseline:
- If they touch it in A2 and the team scores, they gain **+0.94** EVA (High Reward).
- If they touch it in A2 and the team turns it over, they lose **-0.06** EVA (Low Risk).

An average player will score exactly 0.00. Players with high positive scores are mathematically proven to generate scoring chains from positions where the rest of the league fails.
